# Unified Balance

Manage USDC across multiple chains with a single unified balance powered by Circle.

## What is Unified Balance?

Circle's unified balance allows you to deposit USDC once and spend it across supported chains without manually bridging. Your bot can move funds efficiently as needed.

## Features

- **One Balance** - View and manage USDC across 12+ chains from one place
- **Deposit** - Move USDC into unified balance
- **Spend Anywhere** - Pay from unified balance on any supported chain
- **No Manual Bridging** - Circle handles cross-chain settlement

## Using with Your Bot

- "Check my unified balance" - View available USDC across chains
- "Deposit USDC to unified balance" - Move funds in
- "Pay 100 USDC to freelancer" - Pay from unified balance with approval
