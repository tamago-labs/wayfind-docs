# Circle USDC Payments

The Circle USDC Payments template integrates with Circle App Kits to enable cross-chain USDC payments, unified balance management, and automated financial workflows for your Grok Bot.

**[Import Template](https://x.ai/bot/IX2bDYMLKqQhe1hYFnXdi)**

## Overview

Turn your bot into a 24/7 onchain operator for payments and treasury management. Move USDC across 10+ chains with a unified balance, swap tokens, earn yield, and automate payroll workflows - all with human approval.

## Key Features

- **Unified Balance** - Manage USDC across 12+ chains with Circle's unified balance
- **Cross-Chain Transfers** - Send and receive USDC and EURC seamlessly
- **Token Swaps** - Swap tokens via Circle Swap Kit
- **Earn Yield** - Access yield opportunities through vaults like Morpho
- **Multi-Agent Workflows** - CFO, HR, Research, Marketing bots coordinate together
- **Payroll Automation** - Track freelancers, schedule payments, generate onchain payslips

## Supported Networks

Arc, Ethereum, Solana, Base, Arbitrum, Polygon, Avalanche, Optimism, and more.

## Use Cases

- **Payroll Automation** - HR tracks payments, CFO executes with approval
- **Treasury Management** - Research monitors yields, CFO allocates funds
- **Marketing Coordination** - Research gathers insights, Marketing publishes content
- **Cross-Chain Operations** - Move funds efficiently across networks
- **Autonomous Payments** - Schedule recurring payments 24/7

## Proven on Arc Mainnet

Wayfind bots have executed real transactions on Arc Mainnet:
- EURC → USDC swaps via Circle App Kit
- USDC deposits to Unified Balance
- USDC payments from Unified Balance

## Getting Started

1. [Import the template](https://x.ai/bot/IX2bDYMLKqQhe1hYFnXdi) to Grok Bot
2. Let your bot guide wallet setup for Arc/Mainnet or Testnet
3. Configure with Circle App Kits
4. Start building your bot team and automating workflows
