# Explore & Token Details

Discover and research tokenized stocks across all supported issuers and representations.

## Explore Page

Browse the full catalog of tokenized stocks with:
- Price and 24h change
- Market cap and volume
- Available representations
- Issuer information

## Token Detail Pages

For each stock, view detailed information:
- **All Representations** - Compare TSLAx, TSLAon, rTSLA side by side
- **Price Charts** - Historical price data via OHLCV
- **Market Data** - Market cap, volume, supply
- **Issuer Info** - Which company issued which representation
- **Liquidity** - Trading liquidity across DEXes
- **Contract Details** - Token addresses on different chains

## Using with Your Bot

Your bot can help research tokens:
- "List all stock tokens relating to TSLA" - Find all Tesla representations
- "Compare TSLAx vs TSLAon" - See price and liquidity differences
- "Show me details for Apple tokenized stock" - Get full token information
- "What's the best liquidity for NVDA?" - Find optimal trading pair

## Data Sources

Market data is aggregated from multiple sources including CoinMarketCap and onchain data.
