# AI Risk Review

Get personalized AI-powered risk analysis for your tokenized stock portfolio based on your goals and risk tolerance.

## How It Works

### 1. Build Your Risk Profile
Answer a short AI questionnaire about your goals, time horizon, and risk tolerance. This helps personalize the analysis to your needs.

### 2. Get Your API Key
Your API key links your risk profile to your bot setup. One setup works across all your bots.

### 3. Run Risk Review
The AI Risk Engine analyzes your portfolio holdings against market conditions and your risk profile, providing:
- Overall risk score and label
- Factor breakdown (concentration, volatility, liquidity, issuer risk, etc.)
- Specific risks identified
- Actionable recommendations

### 4. Chat Further
After the review, continue the conversation with the multi-agent system. Ask follow-up questions about specific risks, get deeper analysis, or discuss rebalancing strategies.

## Risk Factors Considered

- **Concentration Risk** - Over-allocation to single stocks or sectors
- **Liquidity Risk** - Token liquidity and trading volume
- **Issuer Risk** - Different issuers have different risk profiles
- **Representation Risk** - Multiple versions of same stock with different characteristics
- **Market Conditions** - Volatility and correlation

Risk reviews consume AI credits. New users get 1,000 free credits. Credits are deducted based on token usage ($0.01 per token input+output).
