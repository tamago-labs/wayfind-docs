# Solana Tokenized Stocks

The Solana Tokenized Stocks template equips your Grok Bot with market data, risk intelligence, and trading tools to manage tokenized equity portfolios on Solana and across EVM chains.

**[Import Template](https://x.ai/bot/EpQzEq9pQvX_utfgsxzbj)**

## Overview

Tokenized equities are growing rapidly, but managing them requires understanding multiple representations of the same stock, different issuers, liquidity, and risk factors. This template gives your bot everything it needs to evaluate, track, and trade tokenized stocks intelligently.

## Key Features

- **Multi-Representation Resolution** - Handles stocks like Tesla with multiple onchain versions (TSLAx, TSLAon, rTSLA)
- **Portfolio Management** - Track real holdings or create simulated portfolios
- **AI Risk Review** - Get personalized risk analysis based on your profile and goals
- **OKX DEX Trading** - Execute swaps through OKX DEX Router with approval workflows
- **Market Data** - Access prices, liquidity, market cap, and historical data
- **Multi-Chain Support** - Works across Solana, Ethereum, Arbitrum, and BNB Chain

## Use Cases

- **Portfolio Tracking** - Monitor your tokenized stock holdings across wallets
- **Risk Assessment** - Get AI-powered risk analysis for your portfolio
- **Equity Research** - Research tokenized stocks with market context
- **Automated Rebalancing** - Have your bot prepare rebalancing trades for approval
- **Investment Analysis** - Evaluate opportunities with real-time data

## Getting Started

1. [Import the template](https://x.ai/bot/EpQzEq9pQvX_utfgsxzbj) to Grok Bot
2. Get your API key from [wayfind.click](https://wayfind.click/) dashboard
3. Let your bot guide you through setup and wallet creation
4. Start by asking for a portfolio review or exploring tokenized stocks
