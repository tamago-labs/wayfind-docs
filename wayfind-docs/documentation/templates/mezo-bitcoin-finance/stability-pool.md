# Stability Pool & Staking

Earn yield by staking MUSD in the Stability Pool.

## What is the Stability Pool?

The Stability Pool provides liquidity to absorb liquidations. When troves are liquidated, Stability Pool depositors receive the liquidated collateral (BTC) at a discount.

## How It Works

1. **Deposit MUSD** - Stake your MUSD in the Stability Pool
2. **Earn Rewards** - Receive liquidation rewards in BTC
3. **Withdraw Anytime** - Withdraw MUSD and accumulated rewards

## Benefits

- **Passive Yield** - Earn from liquidation rewards
- **Backed by Protocol** - Helps maintain system stability
- **Flexible** - Deposit and withdraw anytime

## Using with Your Bot

- "Stake 1000 MUSD in Stability Pool" - Deposit to earn yield
- "Check my Stability Pool balance" - View deposits and rewards
- "Claim rewards" - Collect earned BTC
- "Withdraw from Stability Pool" - Remove funds
- "Compare borrowing vs staking" - See which strategy fits better
