# Mezo Bitcoin Finance - Overview

The Mezo Bitcoin Finance template equips your Grok Bot to run Bitcoin finance operations on Mezo testnet. Borrow MUSD against BTC collateral, stake for yield, monitor positions, and automate payments 24/7.

## Key Features

- **Borrow MUSD** - Open troves with BTC collateral, borrow at fixed 1% interest
- **Stability Pool Staking** - Earn yield by staking MUSD in the Stability Pool
- **Position Monitoring** - Track ICR, collateralization, liquidation risk 24/7
- **Automated Management** - Auto-collateralization when ICR drops below threshold
- **MUSD Payments** - Schedule recurring payments and generate onchain payslips
- **Health Alerts** - Get warnings before liquidation risk

## How MUSD Works

MUSD is Mezo's USD-pegged stablecoin, backed by Bitcoin collateral. It's programmable and can be used for payments, staking, or moved across chains.

**Borrowing Path:** Deposit BTC → Open Trove → Mint MUSD (min 110% collateralization ratio)

## Bot Use Cases

- **Position Monitoring & Alerts** - Track ICR and BTC price, alert on risks
- **Automated Position Management** - Add collateral automatically to maintain safe ratios
- **Bitcoin-Powered Payroll** - Pay contributors in MUSD on schedule
- **Yield Optimization** - Move between borrowing strategies and Stability Pool
- **24/7 Operation** - Bots monitor and act even when you're away

## MUSD Utility

- **Payroll** - Pay teams and freelancers in USD-pegged stablecoin
- **Automated Payments** - Recurring payments, subscriptions, service fees
- **Staking** - Earn liquidation rewards in Stability Pool
- **Cross-Chain** - Move MUSD across supported chains
