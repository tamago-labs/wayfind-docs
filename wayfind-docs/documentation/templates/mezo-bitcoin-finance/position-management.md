# Position Management

Monitor and automate your trove positions 24/7.

## Key Metrics

- **ICR (Individual Collateralization Ratio)** - Your collateralization level. Must stay above 110%
- **Collateral** - Amount of BTC deposited
- **Debt** - Amount of MUSD borrowed
- **Liquidation Risk** - Risk of liquidation if ICR drops too low

## Monitoring

Your bot can continuously monitor:
- **ICR Alerts** - Warn when collateralization drops near 110%
- **BTC Price Monitoring** - Track price impact on positions
- **Health Checks** - Regular status reports

## Automation

- **Auto-Collateralization** - Automatically add BTC when ICR drops below threshold
- **Risk Management** - Proactive position adjustments
- **24/7 Monitoring** - Bots watch positions around the clock

## Using with Your Bot

- "Check my trove status" - Get current position details
- "Set ICR alert at 120%" - Configure warnings
- "Auto-add collateral if ICR < 115%" - Set up automation
- "Monitor BTC price impact" - Analyze liquidation risk
- "Show liquidation scenarios" - Calculate risk levels
